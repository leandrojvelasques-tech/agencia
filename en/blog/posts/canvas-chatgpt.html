Canvas: The ChatGPT feature that lets you edit faster and neater

Leandro Velasques

AI Consultant

calendar_today Mar 18, 2026 schedule 4 min read

If you use ChatGPT to write texts, edit code or create content, there's a feature you're probably not taking full advantage of yet: Canvas. In this video, I explain how it works and why it can change the way you work with artificial intelligence.

What is Canvas?

Canvas is a visual interface within ChatGPT that allows you to work with text documents and code in a dedicated editor. Unlike the traditional chat where everything happens linearly, Canvas offers you a workspace where you can edit, reorganize and refine your content in a more intuitive way.

Canvas turns ChatGPT into a true editing assistant, not just a text generator.

Key advantages for professionals

  • In-context editing: You can select parts of the text and ask ChatGPT to modify them without altering the rest of the document.
  • Visual organization: All your content in a clean, structured space, ideal for long documents.
  • Fast iteration: Refine your content progressively, making precise adjustments without repeating full prompts.
  • Code and text: Works for both professional writing and software development.

How to get started?

In the video, I show you the step-by-step process to activate Canvas and the best tips to get the most out of it from your very first use. It's one of those features that, once you try it, you never go back to the conventional chat mode.
